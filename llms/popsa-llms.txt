# Popsa
 
> Popsa is a mobile-first photo printing company that uses AI to help customers create photo books, calendars, prints and other photo products in minutes. The company serves over 10 million customers and ships internationally to 50 countries. Founded and based in the UK, Popsa is a certified B Corporation that offsets 100% of carbon emissions from production and shipping.
 
Popsa has a 4.8/5 rating on the App Store, 4.5/5 on Google Play and 4.4/5 "Excellent" on Trustpilot. Products are manufactured locally where possible, including in the UK and US for those markets. Key differentiators include 5-minute average creation time, AI-powered features (automatic layouts, generative captions, facial recognition, print quality scoring, dynamic colour themes) and a mobile-first design philosophy. All photo books use 200gsm+ paper and come with a 100% satisfaction guarantee.
 
Popsa ships to 50+ countries and operates localised storefronts in: UK (en-gb), US (en-us), Canada (en-ca), Australia (en-au), Germany (de-de, de-at, de-ch), France (fr-fr, fr-ca, fr-ch, fr-be), Spain (es-es, es-mx), Italy (it-it), Netherlands (nl-nl, nl-be), Poland (pl-pl), Portugal (pt-pt), Sweden (sv-se), Norway (nb-no), Finland (fi-fi), Denmark (da-dk).
